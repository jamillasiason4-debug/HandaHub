KNOW WHAT TO DO

Disaster Safety Guides

Remember these basic safety steps during emergencies.

🌀

Typhoon

Monitor official weather updates, prepare supplies, and stay indoors when conditions become dangerous.

🌊

Flood

Move to higher ground when needed. Never walk or drive through floodwater.

🌎

Earthquake

Drop, Cover, and Hold On. After shaking stops, move carefully to a safe open area if necessary.

🔥

Fire

Leave the building using a safe exit. Do not use elevators, and call local emergency services.

Follow instructions from your local authorities and emergency responders.
