KNOW WHAT TO DO

Disaster safety guides

Review basic preparedness reminders. Always follow current instructions from official authorities during an actual emergency.

🌧️HEAVY RAIN & WIND
BEFORE · DURING · AFTER

Typhoon

Prepare for strong winds, heavy rain, flooding, and possible power interruptions.

Read safety reminders
  • Monitor official weather bulletins and local advisories.
  • Secure loose outdoor items and prepare essential supplies before conditions worsen.
  • Stay indoors away from windows during strong winds.
  • Evacuate promptly if authorities instruct you to do so.
  • Avoid downed power lines and report hazards to the proper authorities.
🌊RISING WATER
BEFORE · DURING · AFTER

Flood

Know your route to higher ground and avoid moving water.

Read safety reminders
  • Know whether your area is flood-prone and identify safer routes.
  • Move to higher ground when instructed or when water rises dangerously.
  • Never walk, swim, or drive through floodwater.
  • Keep away from electrical equipment and submerged wires.
  • Return only when authorities say it is safe.
🏠GROUND SHAKING
BEFORE · DURING · AFTER

Earthquake

Practice protective actions and prepare for possible aftershocks.

Read safety reminders
  • Secure heavy furniture and identify safer places in each room.
  • During shaking, Drop, Cover, and Hold On.
  • If indoors, stay inside and protect your head and neck.
  • After shaking stops, check for injuries and hazards before moving.
  • Be prepared for aftershocks and follow official instructions.
🔥FIRE SAFETY
PREVENT · ESCAPE · REPORT

Fire

Plan escape routes and leave quickly if a fire occurs.

Read safety reminders
  • Keep exits clear and identify two ways out where possible.
  • If there is a fire, leave immediately and stay low if smoke is present.
  • Never use elevators during a fire.
  • Once outside, stay out and call the verified local fire service.
  • Do not re-enter until authorities say it is safe.

These are general educational reminders, not a substitute for official instructions or professional training. Verify current guidance with your local disaster risk reduction and management office and relevant government agencies.

ABOUT THE PROJECT

Prepared communities start with informed households.

HandaHub is a student web application concept designed to make basic disaster preparedness easier to organize and understand.

H
Be Ready. Stay Safe.
OUR PURPOSE

Make preparation easier to start.

The application brings household checklists, general safety reminders, and community contact directories together in one simple interface.

It is intended for educational planning. It does not provide live alerts, verify submitted information automatically, or replace official emergency services.

Core objectives

  • Help households track essential emergency supplies.
  • Encourage users to learn basic hazard safety practices.
  • Organize local evacuation and emergency contact information.
  • Promote verification of details with official authorities.
